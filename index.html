Your Experience Matters

An IPAF category tells us what you're qualified for.
Your experience tells us more.

Two Operators can hold the same IPAF category but have very different practical experience. PAC is intended to record both formal qualifications and the actual machines and environments you have worked with.

PAC should never encourage an Operator to accept work on a machine they do not feel competent to operate. Your profile helps us make better matches before that situation arises.

Built Around Safe Operation

Doing the safe thing should never count against you.

PAC wants professional judgement and responsible powered- access operation to be part of an Operator's reputation, not something that is undermined by commercial pressure.

A genuine refusal to operate because conditions are unsafe should be treated as a professional safety decision — not a no-show.

Operators should be able to stop and report:
✓
Machine defects or concerns identified during appropriate pre-use checks.
✓
Ground conditions that appear unsuitable for safe operation.
✓
Missing or inadequate site arrangements that materially affect safe MEWP operation.
✓
Requests to operate beyond machine limits or outside the Operator's competence.
✓
Accidents, near misses and significant safety events requiring PAC attention.
