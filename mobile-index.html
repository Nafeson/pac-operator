Built Around Safe Operation

Doing the safe thing should never count against you.

PAC wants professional judgement and responsible powered-access operation to form part of an Operator's reputation.

A genuine refusal to operate because conditions are unsafe should be treated as a professional safety decision — not a no-show.

Operators should be able to stop and report:
✓
Machine defects or concerns identified during appropriate pre-use checks.
✓
Ground conditions that appear unsuitable for safe operation.
✓
Missing or inadequate site arrangements that materially affect safe MEWP operation.
✓
Requests to operate beyond machine limits or outside the Operator's competence.
✓
Accidents, near misses and significant safety events requiring PAC attention.
